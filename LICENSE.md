- **Community:** free installation and internal use, including use by companies;

- **Official full distribution:** free download, installation, internal copying,

- **Scale activation:** separate commercial rights to additional execution capacity,

supplied versions.

opportunity to read them constitutes acceptance as specified in each agreement;
no separate express confirmation is required. Acceptance alone does not create
a paid Scale order or a payment obligation.

Provide the agreements before installation, startup or use and identify release
contents in the release documentation. Complete each paid Scale order's licensee,
price, term and supplied features before sale. Blank paid-order fields do not limit
the free Community-mode grant. The linked agreements contain the operative terms;
this notice summarizes them and does not itself record acceptance by a customer.

must accompany the
full distribution. Community components retain their Community terms when copied
